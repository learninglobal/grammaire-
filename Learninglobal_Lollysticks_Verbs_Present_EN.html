How does the game work?

Each round has 10 challenges. Earn one point for a correct first attempt without help. If you make a mistake, you can try again as often as you like. The hint shows the forms with je, nous and ils. At the end, you can practise the combinations you found difficult. There is no timer.

The tracks with one, two or three stems refer to the written stems in the present tense. Very common irregular verbs have a separate track. The A1–B2 labels indicate approximate vocabulary difficulty for this activity, rather than an official or exhaustive CEFR list. All tracks practise the same tense: le présent de l’indicatif.

Accents matter! Use the character buttons if needed. Both paie/paye and essaie/essaye are accepted, as are the spellings connaît/connait. Capital letters and spaces before or after your answer do not affect marking. The game works offline and does not send your answers anywhere.
